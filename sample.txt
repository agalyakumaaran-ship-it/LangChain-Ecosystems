Pfizer Inc. is a multinational pharmaceutical and biotechnology corporation founded in 1849 by German-American cousins Charles Pfizer and Charles F. Erhart in New York City. The company is headquartered in New York and is known for producing numerous blockbuster drugs and vaccines. 

Albert Bourla serves as the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Pfizer. In recent history, Pfizer acquired Seagen, a biotechnology company specializing in cancer therapies, for approximately 43 billion dollars in 2023. Additionally, Pfizer partnered with BioNTech to develop a widely distributed mRNA-based COVID-19 vaccine.
